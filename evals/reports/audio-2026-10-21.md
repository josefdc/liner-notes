# Audio accuracy, 2026-10-21

Tempo and key accuracy of the audio service (`services/audio`) on annotated datasets, measured by `evals/audio` (`uv run python -m evals_audio`). The harness imports the service and calls its own `analyze_signals` on 30-second clips, so the numbers are what the service returns, gate and withholding rules included. Rendered from `audio-2026-10-21.json`, which holds the per-track results.

This report covers the tempo and key suites with the librosa and Essentia estimators. The estimator bake-off with beat-this, style hit@k against Discogs styles and the agreement with Deezer's `bpm` come in the next part of this work and are not here.

## How to read it

- **n** is every selected track with verified audio and a usable reference.
- **Withheld**: the service returned no tempo (or no key) for the clip. Withheld tracks are counted on their own: they are not scored as wrong and they stay in n. Accuracy is over the answered tracks; coverage is answered / n.
- **Failed**: the analysis raised an error (listed per run). None is expected.
- **Acc1**: the tempo is within 4 % of the reference. **Acc2**: within 4 % of the reference times 1, 2, 3, 1/2 or 1/3 (octave and meter errors forgiven).
- **MIREX** key score: 1 same key, 0.5 a perfect fifth above in the same mode, 0.3 relative, 0.2 parallel, 0 otherwise. **exact**: the same key only.
- **95 % CI**: percentile bootstrap over the answered tracks (10,000 resamples, fixed seed). With n = 50 the intervals are wide; read them before the means.
- **Coverage curve**: answered tracks sorted by the service's own confidence (tempo `confidence`, key `strength`), and the accuracy of the most confident ones at each share of n. A useful confidence makes accuracy fall as coverage grows.

## Reproducing

Datasets are fetched into `~/liner-notes-data` (or `LINER_NOTES_DATA`), outside the repository, and never exceed `LINER_NOTES_DATA_CAP_BYTES` (2 GB by default). Only the selected tracks' audio is downloaded, each checked against mirdata's MD5. See `evals/audio/README.md`.
